Methodology

EuroLeague

PACE is estimated as FGA + 0.44×FTA - OREB + TOV and averaged with opponent possessions. Next-five difficulty uses opponent pace, PIR allowed, points allowed, FGA allowed and rebounds allowed. Player option score combines projected PIR, value, minutes, shot usage, schedule and injury risk.

FPL

FPL tables use public player, fixture and element history data. Value is points per game divided by price. Minutes are tracked as average per appearance, per gameweek and recent 3/5-game trends.

All models are beta estimates and should support, not replace, your own fantasy judgment.
