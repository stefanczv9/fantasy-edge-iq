Methodology

Fantasy Edge IQ uses public fantasy and sports data to build practical decision-support indicators. The models are beta estimates, not guarantees.

EuroLeague Fantasy

  • Team pace: estimated as FGA + 0.44 × FTA - OREB + TOV, blended with opponent possessions where available.
  • Next-five difficulty: built from opponent pace, PIR allowed, points allowed, FGA allowed and rebounds allowed. Lower FDR is easier.
  • Option score: combines projected PIR, value, minutes, shooting usage, price gap, form, schedule context and risk penalties.
  • Lineup IQ: compares your selected players with same-position affordable replacements using price, bank, schedule and option score.

Fantasy Premier League

  • FPL dashboards use public player, fixture, price, ownership, transfer, injury/news, minutes and expected-points data.
  • FPL Transfer IQ analyzes the latest publicly available squad for a Team ID and suggests affordable same-position ideas.
  • Transfer suggestions consider price, bank, projection, next-five fixture difficulty, player status/news and a simple minutes/security score.

Domestic league data

Domestic league form may be added later as a separate context layer. It will not be mixed directly into EuroLeague numbers.
