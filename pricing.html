Pricing

Fantasy Edge IQ is free during beta.

During beta, managers can use the EuroLeague dashboard, EuroLeague Lineup IQ, FPL dashboard and FPL Transfer IQ without payment.

Possible future Pro features

  • Saved watchlists across devices
  • Transfer and price-change alerts
  • Lineup builders and squad optimizers
  • Private projections and model presets
  • Mini-league or team-history analysis

No paid plan is active right now. If a paid version launches later, beta users will be told clearly before any charge exists.

Last updated: 2026-09-25. Fantasy Edge IQ is an independent fantasy analytics project and is not affiliated with Euroleague Basketball, the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League. Analytics are beta estimates, not guarantees.
