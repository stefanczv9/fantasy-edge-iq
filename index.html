FAQ

Is Fantasy Edge IQ free?

Yes. The platform is in free beta this season. A Pro version with watchlists, alerts and lineup tools may come later.

Is this affiliated with EuroLeague, Premier League or FPL?

No. Fantasy Edge IQ is an independent analytics project and is not affiliated with Euroleague Basketball, the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.

Does the dashboard give guaranteed picks?

No model can guarantee fantasy outcomes. The dashboards provide context, rankings and risk indicators so managers can make better decisions.
