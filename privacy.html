Privacy Policy

Fantasy Edge IQ is a beta analytics website. We aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

What we process

  • When you use FPL Transfer IQ, the Team ID you enter is sent to our API so the tool can request public FPL data.
  • EuroLeague Lineup IQ stores selected players locally in your browser if you click save.
  • Analytics may collect anonymous usage information such as page views, device type and country-level traffic once analytics is enabled.

What we do not ask for

  • We do not ask for your FPL password.
  • We do not ask for your EuroLeague Fantasy password.
  • We do not sell personal data.

This policy will be updated if accounts, payments or advanced feedback tools are added later.

Last updated: 2026-09-25. Fantasy Edge IQ is an independent fantasy analytics project and is not affiliated with Euroleague Basketball, the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League. Analytics are beta estimates, not guarantees.
