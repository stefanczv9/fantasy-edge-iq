Method

  • Team pace: estimated as FGA + 0.44 × FTA - OREB + TOV, blended with opponent possessions where available.
  • Next-five difficulty: uses opponent pace, PIR allowed, points allowed, FGA allowed and rebounds allowed. Lower FDR is easier.
  • Option score: combines projected PIR, value, minutes, shooting usage, price gap, form, schedule and risk penalties.
  • Full stats tab: includes all 81 columns currently generated by the model, not only the short decision view.

All metrics are beta estimates and should support, not replace, your own fantasy judgment.
